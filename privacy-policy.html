PocketTools Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

PocketTools is a free, ad-supported mobile app with tools for QR codes, images and PDF files. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information. PocketTools is developed by [YOUR NAME].

No account required

PocketTools does not ask you to create an account or sign in. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal information. There is no user profile.

Your files stay on your device

All image, PDF and QR code processing happens on your phone. The photos, PDFs and other files you open or create with PocketTools are never uploaded to a server. We do not access, read, store or transmit your files.

When you save a result to your gallery or share it with the share button, the file is written to your own device storage or sent only to the app you choose.

Information stored on your device

PocketTools may keep a small amount of data only on your device, such as your QR scan history and your app settings (for example, light or dark theme). This data never leaves your device. You can clear your history in the app's Settings, or remove everything by uninstalling the app.

Permissions

PocketTools asks only for the permissions a tool needs, at the moment you use it:

  • Camera: used to scan QR codes. No photo or video is recorded or stored.
  • Photos and files: used to let you choose images and PDFs to process, and to save results to your gallery.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob to show and measure ads (see "Ads" below).

PocketTools does not request access to your microphone, location, contacts or notifications.

Ads

PocketTools is free and supported by ads. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, a third-party service. AdMob may collect and use device and usage information, such as your advertising ID, IP address and approximate location, to show and measure ads. This information is handled by Google under Google's policies on how it uses information from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Where required by law (for example, in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), PocketTools shows a consent form before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy choices.

You can also manage ad personalization in your device settings, and reset or delete your advertising ID there (on Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Analytics

PocketTools does not currently send analytics. If analytics is added in the future, it will be limited to aggregate, non-personal usage data. It will never include your files or their contents, and this policy will be updated.

Children's privacy

PocketTools is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will address it.

Data security

Because your files are processed only on your device and are not sent to us, we do not hold your files or personal data on any server.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last changed. Material changes will be reflected in the app's store listing or in the app.
