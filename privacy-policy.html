# PocketTools — Privacy Policy Last updated: October 2026 ## No account required PocketTools does not ask you to create an account. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal information. There is no sign-in and no user profile. ## Your files stay on your device All image and PDF processing happens locally on your phone. Photos, PDFs, QR codes, and any other files you open or create with PocketTools are processed on your device and are never uploaded to a server. We do not access, read, store, or transmit your files. If you save a result to your gallery or share it using the share button, that file is written to your own device storage or sent only to the app you choose to share with. ## Device permissions PocketTools asks only for what a tool needs, at the moment you use it: - **Camera** — used to scan QR codes. No photo or video is recorded. - **Photos** — used to let you choose images to process and to save results to your gallery. - **Advertising ID** — used by Google AdMob to serve and measure ads (see Ads below). PocketTools does not request access to your microphone, location, contacts, or notifications. ## Ads PocketTools is free and ad-supported. Ads are served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device and usage information (such as advertising ID and approximate location) to show and measure ads, as described in Google's privacy policy. You can manage ad personalization in your device settings. ## Optional analytics PocketTools does not currently send analytics. If analytics is added in the future, it will be optional and limited to aggregate, non-personal app usage data. No personal information or file content will be included. ## Children PocketTools is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. ## Contact If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us through the app store listing for PocketTools.
